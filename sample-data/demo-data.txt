Sample data for the AI Recipe & Meal Planner
=============================================

The app starts with no saved recipes. Run it with --fixtures (see README) to
start with the 26 recipes in fixtures.json, whose names differ from the ones
below. To load the recipes below, use
"Add personal recipe" (GUI: Recipes tab; CLI: main menu option 2) and paste
one recipe at a time. In the CLI, type END on its own line after the recipe.
The recipes are written in different styles on purpose, to show the AI
organising each one into the same fields.


--- Recipe 1 ---
Garlic Fried Rice
Dinner, takes about 20 minutes, serves 2.
Ingredients: 2 cups cooked rice (day-old is best), 2 eggs, 3 cloves garlic, 2 green onions, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vegetable oil.
1. Heat the oil and fry the minced garlic until golden.
2. Push it aside, scramble the eggs, then add the rice.
3. Stir in the soy sauce and sliced green onions and fry for 3 more minutes.


--- Recipe 2 ---
Overnight Oats (breakfast, serves 1, 5 min prep + overnight)
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1/2 cup berries
Mix everything except the berries in a jar, refrigerate overnight, and top with the berries in the morning.


--- Recipe 3 ---
Chickpea Spinach Curry - vegetarian dinner for 4, 30 minutes.
You need 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp curry powder, 1 can chickpeas (drained), 1 can coconut milk, 3 cups spinach and salt.
Soften the chopped onion and garlic in a little oil, stir in the curry powder for a minute, add the chickpeas and coconut milk and simmer for 15 minutes, then wilt in the spinach and season with salt. Serve with rice.


--- Recipe 4 ---
Greek Salad
Lunch | 10 min | serves 2
Ingredients:
  cucumber - 1
  tomatoes - 3
  red onion - 1/2
  feta - 100 g
  kalamata olives - 1/3 cup
  olive oil - 3 tbsp
  dried oregano - 1 tsp
Steps:
  Chop the vegetables, add the olives and cubed feta, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with oregano.


--- Recipe 5 ---
Banana Pancakes
Breakfast. Makes 8 small pancakes (serves 2). 15 minutes.
2 ripe bananas, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup flour, 1 tsp baking powder, a pinch of salt, butter for the pan.
Mash the bananas, whisk in the eggs, then stir in the flour, baking powder and salt. Cook spoonfuls in a buttered pan for about 2 minutes per side.

Suggested demo inputs
=====================

Profile (F11): Dietary restrictions - vegetarian; Disliked ingredients - mushrooms;
  Nutrition goals - high protein. To show the nonsense check, set
  Other preferences to "purple monkey dishwasher" and then run an AI feature.

  then "Apply that"

  "saved recipes only"
